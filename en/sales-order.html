A sales order (or order confirmation) is the document the seller sends back to a customer to acknowledge an order and confirm the details before fulfilling it. It is the mirror image of a purchase order: the buyer issues a PO, and you reply with a sales order quoting their PO number, the agreed items and prices, the ship-to address and the delivery date.

What to include on a sales order

  • Your business details and a unique sales order number
  • The customer and their billing address
  • The ship-to address and the expected delivery date
  • The customer’s PO number for cross-reference
  • Itemised lines with agreed quantities and unit prices, tax and the order total

Sales order vs purchase order vs invoice

The purchase order is raised by the buyer to place the order. The sales order is your acknowledgement that you accept it and will supply on the stated terms. The invoice comes later, once the goods or services are delivered, to request payment. Keeping the same number thread across all three lets both sides match the paperwork. Frequently asked questions

What is a sales order?

A sales order (or order confirmation) is the seller’s acknowledgement of a customer’s order. It confirms the items, agreed prices, ship-to address and delivery date, and references the customer’s PO number so both sides can match the paperwork.

What is the difference between a sales order and a purchase order?

The buyer issues the purchase order to place the order; the seller replies with a sales order to confirm it will supply on those terms. The invoice comes later to request payment. A sales order references the buyer’s PO number.

Is an order confirmation the same as a sales order?

In most small businesses, yes — both acknowledge an accepted order and confirm its details before fulfilment. Use whichever term your customers expect; the layout is the same.

Can I create an invoice from a sales order?

Yes. Once you have fulfilled the order, switch to “Invoice” under the preview — the items, customer and references carry over with a new invoice number.
