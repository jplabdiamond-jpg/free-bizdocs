Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Free BizDoc ("we", "us") provides free online tools for creating business documents such as invoices, estimates, receipts, purchase orders and packing slips. This policy explains what data we handle and how. It applies to the English pages under free-bizdocs.com/en/.

In short: the documents you create are generated entirely in your browser and are never uploaded to our servers. We only receive data you actively send us (for example, a contact message), plus minimal technical data needed to keep the free tools available.

1. Document content is not sent to us

When you fill in a document and download the PDF, all processing — including the PDF generation and any logo you add — happens locally in your browser. The content of your documents (client names, line items, amounts, bank details, logos) is never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

2. Data stored in your own browser

To save you re-typing, your business details, theme and an unsent draft are stored in your browser's local storage on your device only. This data stays on your device, is not accessible to us, and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings or by using the "Clear" option in the tool.

3. Information you send us

  • Contact form: if you use our contact form, we store the name, email address and message you submit so we can reply and improve the service.

4. Technical & anti-abuse data

  • Rate limiting: to keep the free tools available to everyone, we record a coarse, network-level identifier (derived from your IP address) together with a timestamp when a free PDF is created. This is used only to enforce the hourly free limit and is deleted automatically.
  • Language preference: a small cookie named site_lang remembers whether you chose the Japanese or English version, so we don't redirect you against your choice.
  • Standard server logs: our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes requests and may keep standard, short-lived access logs for security and reliability.

5. Cookies & tracking

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics/tracking on these pages. The only cookie we set is the language-preference cookie described above.

6. Third parties

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and processes network requests.
  • The PDF is produced in your browser using an open-source library loaded from a public CDN; no document data is sent to the CDN.

7. Data retention & your rights

We keep contact messages only as long as needed to handle your enquiry. You may ask us to access or delete the information you submitted via the contact form by writing to us. Because document content never reaches us, there is nothing on our side to delete for the documents you create.

8. Children

The service is intended for business use and is not directed at children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the latest revision.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Please use our contact form.
