A purchase order (PO) is issued by the buyer to a supplier to confirm what is being ordered, at what price, and where and when it should be delivered. Once the supplier accepts it, the PO number is referenced on their invoice and packing slip so the three documents can be matched.

What to include on a purchase order

  • Your company details (the buyer) and a unique PO number
  • The vendor name and address
  • The ship-to address and required delivery date
  • Itemised lines with quantities and agreed unit prices
  • Tax and the order total, plus any terms (payment terms, delivery instructions)
  • An authorisation signature line

Frequently asked questions

Who issues a purchase order - the buyer or the seller?

The buyer. The supplier then quotes the PO number on their invoice and delivery documents.

Is a purchase order a contract?

Once accepted by the supplier it generally forms a binding order for the stated goods, prices and terms. Add your terms in the Terms box if you need specific conditions.
