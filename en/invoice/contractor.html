What a contractor invoice should include

  • Your business and the customer, plus the job/site address
  • Invoice number, date and due date
  • Labor hours, materials, equipment and permit/fees as separate lines
  • Sales tax / VAT / GST where it applies
  • Payment terms and how to pay

Frequently asked questions

What should a contractor invoice include?

Your business and the customer, the job address, a breakdown of labor (hours x rate), materials, equipment and any permit fees, plus tax where it applies and your payment terms.

How do I show labor and materials separately?

Add a line for labor (hours x your rate) and separate lines for materials, equipment and fees, so the customer can see exactly what they are paying for.
