What a Canada invoice must include

  • Your business name and the date
  • A unique invoice number
  • Your GST/HST registration number (required on invoices once registered)
  • A description of the goods or services
  • The GST/HST (and PST/QST where it applies) at your province’s rate, or a statement that tax is included
  • The total - for sales of $150+ more detail is required

Frequently asked questions

How is tax handled across provinces?

Pick your province and the tool applies the correct combination - HST in Ontario and the Atlantic provinces, GST plus PST in BC/Manitoba/Saskatchewan, GST plus QST in Quebec, or GST only in Alberta and the territories.

Do I need my GST/HST number on the invoice?

Yes, once you are registered you must show your GST/HST number on invoices, along with the tax amount (or a statement that it is included) so customers can claim input tax credits.

What rate is used for Ontario?

Ontario uses HST at 13%. The example here uses Ontario; change the province in the tool to apply your own rate.
