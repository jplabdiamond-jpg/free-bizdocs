Frequently asked questions

How do I invoice for recurring cleaning?

List each visit (or a monthly line for the agreed number of visits) at your per-visit rate, add any extras or supplies, and invoice on your regular cycle.

Due on receipt or Net 7?

Both are common for cleaning. “Due on receipt” asks for immediate payment; Net 7 gives a week. Pick the term under the form.
