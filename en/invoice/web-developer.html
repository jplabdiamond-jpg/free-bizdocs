Frequently asked questions

How do web developers invoice clients?

Either a fixed project fee split into milestones, or hourly at your rate. List each as a line item with a clear description, and reference the project or milestone in the notes.

How do I handle milestone billing?

Invoice each milestone separately (deposit, mid-point, launch). Reuse your details with a new invoice number for each, and note which milestone it covers.
