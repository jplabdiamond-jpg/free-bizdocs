Frequently asked questions

What should a graphic design invoice include?

Your studio and client details, an invoice number, each deliverable with its price (or your hourly rate x hours), and your terms - including that final files and usage rights transfer on full payment.

Should I invoice a deposit for design work?

Yes, it is common to invoice 50% up front to begin and the balance on delivery. Create a deposit invoice, then convert it to a final invoice for the remainder.
