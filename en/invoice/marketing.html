Frequently asked questions

What should a marketing invoice include?

Your agency and client, a management retainer line, content or deliverables (per post or per item), and any campaign setup, each priced clearly, plus your monthly terms.

How do I handle ad spend?

Keep ad spend separate from your fees - either the client pays the platform directly, or you add a clearly labelled pass-through line. Do not bury it in your management fee.
