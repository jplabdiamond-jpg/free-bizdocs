What a freelancer invoice should include

  • Your name/business and the client (“Bill To”)
  • A unique invoice number and the issue and due dates
  • Project work or hours with a clear description and your rate
  • Payment terms (Net 14 or Net 30 is common for freelancers)
  • How to pay - bank transfer, card or PayPal

Frequently asked questions

How do I invoice as a freelancer?

Enter your business name, the client, an invoice number, your line items (project fee or hours x rate), and your payment terms, then download the PDF. No sign-up is needed.

Should a freelancer charge a deposit?

For larger projects it is common to invoice a deposit (often 30-50%) up front and the balance on delivery. Create a deposit invoice first, then a final invoice for the remainder.

Do I need to add tax?

Only if you are registered to charge it. Pick your country and set the sales tax / VAT / GST rate, or mark lines exempt.
