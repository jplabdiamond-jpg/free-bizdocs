Frequently asked questions

What goes on a photography invoice?

Your studio and client details, the shoot date, the session fee, edited images (per image or as a package), travel, and any print or usage release, with your deposit and balance terms.

How do I invoice a booking deposit?

Create a deposit invoice for the booking fee, then a final invoice for the balance before you deliver the edited gallery. The details carry over with a new number.
