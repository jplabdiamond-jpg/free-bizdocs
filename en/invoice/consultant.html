Frequently asked questions

How should a consultant structure an invoice?

List your advisory time by day or hour, plus any fixed-fee deliverables or retainer, with your rate shown per line so the client can see how the total is built up.

How do I invoice a retainer?

Add a single “Monthly retainer” line for the agreed amount and invoice it at the start of each period. You can reuse the same details each month with a new invoice number.
