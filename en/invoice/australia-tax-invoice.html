What a Australia invoice must include

  • The words “Tax Invoice” (shown automatically once you add an ABN and charge GST)
  • Your business name and your ABN
  • The date the invoice is issued
  • A description of the items sold, with quantities
  • The GST amount (10%), or a statement that the total price includes GST
  • For sales of $1,000 or more, the buyer’s identity or ABN

Frequently asked questions

What makes an Australian tax invoice valid?

For sales over $82.50 (incl. GST) a tax invoice must show the words “Tax Invoice”, your ABN, the date, a description, and the GST amount (or that the total includes GST). Over $1,000 it must also show the buyer’s identity or ABN.

How does the title become “Tax Invoice”?

Enter your ABN in your business details and keep GST at 10% on the lines - the heading switches to “Tax Invoice” and a “Total price includes GST” note is added.

What if I am not registered for GST?

If you are not GST registered, do not charge GST - set the lines to GST-free. The document stays a plain invoice rather than a tax invoice.
