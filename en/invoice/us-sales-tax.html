What a United States invoice must include

  • Your business name, address and contact details (and EIN if you use one)
  • The word “Invoice”, a unique number and the issue date
  • The customer’s name and address (“Bill To”)
  • Itemized lines with description, quantity, unit price and amount
  • Sales tax applied per line where you have nexus - the rate depends on your state and the product/service
  • Subtotal, tax and total, plus payment terms (Net 30 is common)

Frequently asked questions

Is there a required invoice format in the US?

No. There is no federal standard. Include your details, the customer, a unique invoice number, itemized lines, any sales tax, and the total. Sales tax rules depend on your state and whether you have nexus there.

How do I add sales tax?

Set your state’s sales-tax rate in the tool and mark taxable lines; exempt items can be set to exempt. The PDF shows the tax separately from the subtotal.

What paper size is the PDF?

US invoices download on Letter (8.5 x 11 in) in USD. The tool switches paper, currency and date format when you pick a country.
