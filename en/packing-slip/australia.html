Frequently asked questions

What is a delivery note used for?

It lists the goods and quantities in a delivery, with no prices, so the recipient can confirm the delivery matches their order. It travels with the goods.

Does it show GST?

No - GST goes on the tax invoice. A delivery note shows quantities only, so there is no tax on it.

Is a delivery note the same as a tax invoice?

No - the tax invoice requests payment and shows GST and your ABN; the delivery note confirms what was shipped. Keep the tax invoice for billing.
