Frequently asked questions

What is a packing slip?

A packing slip (or delivery note) lists the items and quantities in a shipment, without prices. It travels with the goods so the recipient can check the delivery against their order.

Why no prices on a packing slip?

Prices belong on the invoice, which goes to the person who pays. The packing slip goes with the goods, so it shows only what was shipped - handy when the receiver is not the buyer.

How is it different from an invoice?

An invoice requests payment and shows prices and tax; a packing slip confirms what was shipped. Keep the matching invoice for billing.
