Frequently asked questions

What is a delivery note?

A delivery note (or despatch note) lists the goods and quantities in a delivery, without prices. It accompanies the goods so the recipient can verify the delivery against their order.

Is a delivery note the same as an invoice?

No - a delivery note confirms what was delivered and shows no prices; the invoice requests payment and shows the VAT. Keep both: one travels with the goods, the other goes for payment.

Does a delivery note need VAT?

No - VAT goes on the VAT invoice. A delivery note lists quantities only, so there is nothing to tax on it.
