What a United States estimate must include

  • Your business name, address and contact details
  • The word “Estimate” (or “Quote”), a number and the date
  • The customer’s name and address (“Prepared For”)
  • Itemized lines with description, quantity and unit price
  • Sales tax shown where you charge it, and the estimated total
  • A valid-until date and an acceptance/signature line

Frequently asked questions

Is an estimate legally binding?

An estimate is a good-faith quote, not a contract, until the customer accepts it. Add a valid-until date so the pricing has a clear expiry, and an acceptance line so approval is recorded.

Does the estimate show sales tax?

Yes - set your state rate and the estimated sales tax is shown separately, so the customer sees the full expected total before they approve.

Can I turn the estimate into an invoice?

Yes - once the customer approves, switch the document to Invoice and the same line items, tax and totals carry over. No retyping.
