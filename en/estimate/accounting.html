Frequently asked questions

What should an accounting quote include?

The recurring services (bookkeeping, payroll) with monthly fees, any one-off work (tax return) as a fixed fee, what is included, a valid-until date, and an acceptance line.

What is the difference between an estimate and a quote?

An estimate is an approximate price that may change; a quote is a fixed price the client can accept, valid for a set period. Say which you are issuing and include a valid-until date. This tool names the document to match your country.

Can I turn the quote into an invoice?

Yes. Once the client accepts, open it in the editor and switch to “Invoice” under the preview - the same line items and client carry over with a new invoice number and today’s date.
