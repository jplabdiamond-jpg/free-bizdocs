What a Canada estimate must include

  • Your business name and your GST/HST number if registered
  • The word “Estimate” (or “Quote”), a number and the date
  • The customer’s name and address
  • A description of the goods or services quoted
  • The GST/HST (and PST/QST where it applies) at your province’s rate, and the total in CAD
  • A valid-until date and an acceptance line

Frequently asked questions

How is the tax set for my province?

Pick your province and the correct combination is applied - HST in Ontario and Atlantic Canada, GST plus PST in BC/Manitoba/Saskatchewan, GST plus QST in Quebec, or GST only in Alberta and the territories.

Does the estimate show my GST/HST number?

Yes - if you are registered, your GST/HST number appears on the estimate, as it will on the invoice when the quote is accepted.

Can the estimate become an invoice?

Yes - switch it to Invoice and the same lines, provincial tax and totals carry across, with no retyping.
