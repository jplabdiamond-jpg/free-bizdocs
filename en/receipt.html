A receipt is proof that a payment was received. Customers need it for expense claims and bookkeeping; you need a copy for your records. This generator produces a simple, professional receipt PDF with everything both sides expect.

What to include on a receipt

  • Your business name, address and tax ID (where applicable)
  • A receipt number and the date of payment
  • Who paid (“Received From”)
  • What was paid for - itemized lines, tax and total
  • The payment method (cash, card, cheque, bank transfer) and a “paid” indication

Receipt vs invoice

An invoice asks for payment; a receipt confirms it. If you issued an invoice first, create the receipt from it with one click - the lines and customer are carried over and the document is marked as paid in full.

Frequently asked questions

What information must a receipt include?

A receipt should show your business name and contact details, a receipt number and the date of payment, who paid, what was paid for, the amount, and the payment method (cash, card, bank transfer). Add tax if you charged it.

Is a receipt proof of purchase?

Yes. A receipt is the document that proves a payment was made — customers use it for expense claims, warranties and bookkeeping, and you keep a copy for your records.

Do receipts need to show tax?

For VAT- or GST-registered businesses, yes - a receipt that doubles as a tax invoice must itemize the tax. In the US, show sales tax if you charged it.

Can I make a receipt from an invoice?

Yes. On the invoice page, click “Receipt” under the preview. The content is reused and marked as paid.
