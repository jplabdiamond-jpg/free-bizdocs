What a United States receipt must include

  • Your business name and contact details

  • The amount paid, the payment method, and a PAID indication

Frequently asked questions

Does a US receipt need to show sales tax?

If you charged sales tax, show it on the receipt as well as the invoice - the customer needs it for their records and you need it for your sales-tax filing. Set your state rate and it is itemized separately.

Is a receipt the same as a sales receipt?

Yes - a sales receipt confirms a completed payment. It shows what was bought, the sales tax, the total paid and the method.
