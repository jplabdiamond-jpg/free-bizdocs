What a Canada receipt must include

  • Your business name and your GST/HST number
  • A receipt number and the date of payment
  • Who paid
  • A description, with the GST/HST (and PST/QST where it applies) by province
  • The amount paid, the method, and a PAID indication

Frequently asked questions

Does a Canadian receipt need the GST/HST number?

Yes - once registered, show your GST/HST number and the tax amount on receipts so customers can claim their input tax credits.

How is the tax set for my province?

Pick your province and the correct GST, HST, PST or QST is applied - for example HST in Ontario, GST plus PST in BC.
