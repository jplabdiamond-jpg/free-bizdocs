Frequently asked questions

Should I give a receipt for every visit?

Give a receipt whenever the customer pays - per visit or monthly. It is their proof of payment and a simple record for you.

What is the difference between a receipt and an invoice?

An invoice requests payment; a receipt confirms it was paid. Issue the invoice first, then a receipt once the money arrives - this tool can turn an invoice into a receipt in one click, marked paid in full.

Do I need to show tax on a receipt?

If you charged sales tax, VAT or GST, itemize it on the receipt too - a receipt that doubles as a tax receipt must show the tax. Pick your country and the tax is calculated and shown separately.
