Frequently asked questions

What is a VAT receipt?

A VAT receipt is proof of payment that also shows the VAT charged and your VAT number, so a VAT-registered customer can reclaim the VAT. It must itemize the VAT rate and amount.

Can a receipt double as a VAT invoice?

For smaller amounts a VAT receipt can serve the same purpose if it shows your VAT number, the date, a description and the VAT. For larger sales issue a full VAT invoice.
