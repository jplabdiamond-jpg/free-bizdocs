Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a sales receipt and an invoice?

An invoice requests payment before it is made; a sales receipt confirms a completed sale and payment. The receipt is the customer’s proof of purchase.

Does a sales receipt show tax?

Yes - if you charge sales tax, VAT or GST, itemize it on the receipt. Pick your country and the tax is calculated and shown separately.

Is a sales receipt proof of purchase?

Yes - it is the document a customer uses for returns, warranties and expense records, showing what was bought, the tax and the total paid.
