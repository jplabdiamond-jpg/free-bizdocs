How to Invoice as a Landscaper or Gardener

Industries · Free BizDoc · __PUBDATE_EN__

Landscaping invoices range from a one-off patio to weekly garden maintenance, and each bills differently. Clear breakdowns and the right setup for recurring work keep you paid. Here is how.

Split labour, materials and plants

  • Your business details and the job / site address
  • Labour (hours or a fixed job price)
  • Materials — aggregates, paving, timber
  • Plants and any delivery or disposal (green waste) fees
  • Tax if registered, a clear total and payment details

One-off projects

For a defined project (a new patio, a planting scheme), quote a fixed price, take a deposit to cover materials up front, and bill the balance on completion. For larger builds, use milestone payments so you’re not funding weeks of materials and labour yourself.

Recurring maintenance

Regular mowing or garden care suits recurring invoices — a fixed monthly fee, or per-visit billing. Set it up to go out on a schedule so you’re not re-creating the same invoice weekly. A standing arrangement also smooths your cash flow across the seasons.

Get paid on time

Invoice promptly — ideally the day a job finishes — with an easy payment option. For maintenance clients, consistent monthly invoicing trains reliable payment. Number every invoice and keep copies for your records and tax.

Frequently asked questions

What should a landscaper's invoice include?

Your business details and the site address, an itemised split of labour, materials, plants and any delivery or green-waste disposal, tax if registered, a clear total and payment details.

How do I invoice for regular garden maintenance?

Use recurring invoices &mdash; a fixed monthly fee or per-visit billing set to go out on a schedule, so you're not recreating the same invoice each week. It also smooths cash flow across seasons.

Should I take a deposit for landscaping jobs?

For one-off projects with upfront material costs, yes &mdash; a deposit covers materials and the balance is due on completion. For larger builds, use milestone payments.
