Can You Invoice Without a Company? (Yes — Here's How)

Invoicing · Free BizDoc · __PUBDATE_EN__

You do not need a registered company to send an invoice. An individual, freelancer or sole trader can invoice a client with a simple, professional document — it just needs the right details. Here is exactly what to include.

You can invoice as an individual

An invoice is just a request for payment. There is no law that says it has to come from a limited company. If you did the work, you can bill for it under your own name. Most freelancers and side-hustlers start exactly this way, before they ever register a company.

What to put on it

  • The word Invoice and a unique invoice number
  • Your full name and contact details (and address, where required)
  • Your tax ID only if you have one — you can leave it off if you don’t
  • The client’s name and address
  • Issue date, due date, and an itemised list of what you are charging for
  • The total, and how to pay (bank details or a payment link)

Do you charge tax?

That depends on where you are and your turnover — not on whether you have a company. If you are not registered for VAT, GST or sales tax, you simply don’t add it, and you don’t show a tax number. Once you cross your country’s registration threshold, you register and start charging it. When in doubt, check your local tax authority or an accountant.

Keep it professional

A clean, numbered invoice gets taken seriously and paid faster, even without a company behind it. Number your invoices in sequence, keep a copy of every one, and send a polite reminder if it goes past the due date.

Frequently asked questions

Can I send an invoice as an individual?

Yes. Anyone can issue an invoice for work they have done. It does not require a registered company. Include your name, contact details, an invoice number, the client, the items, the total and payment details.

Do I need a tax number to invoice?

No. You only show a tax number (VAT/GST/sales tax) if you are registered. Many freelancers and sole traders invoice without one until they reach their country's registration threshold.

Is a handwritten or basic invoice valid?

Yes, as long as it contains the required details. A clear typed PDF looks more professional and is easier for the client to process and pay.
