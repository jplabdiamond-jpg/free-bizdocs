What Does &ldquo;Net 30&rdquo; Mean on an Invoice?

Getting paid · Free BizDoc · __PUBDATE_EN__

Net 30 means the full payment is due within 30 days of the invoice date. It is one of the most common payment terms in business. Here is what it means in practice and how to choose the right term for your invoices.

What Net 30 actually means

The number is the number of days the client has to pay, counted from the invoice date (unless you state otherwise). So an invoice dated the 1st with Net 30 terms is due by the 31st. “Net” refers to the full amount, as opposed to terms that offer an early-payment discount.

Net 15, Net 30, Net 60

  • Net 15 — due in 15 days. Good for smaller clients and faster cash flow.
  • Net 30 — due in 30 days. The common default for business clients.
  • Net 60 — due in 60 days. Often requested by large companies; harder on your cash flow.

Early-payment discounts

You may see terms like 2/10 Net 30: the full amount is due in 30 days, but the client can take a 2% discount if they pay within 10. It is a simple way to encourage faster payment without changing your headline price for everyone.

Which term should you use?

Shorter terms get you paid sooner, so for freelancers and small jobs, Net 14 or Net 15 is often better than Net 30. Always put the actual due date on the invoice as well as the term — a concrete date is clearer than counting days and tends to get paid faster.

Frequently asked questions

Does Net 30 mean 30 business days?

No, it normally means 30 calendar days from the invoice date, unless you specifically state business days. To avoid any doubt, print the exact due date on the invoice.

When does the Net 30 clock start?

Usually from the invoice date. Some businesses count from the delivery date or end of month instead, so state which you mean if it is not the invoice date.

Is Net 30 good for freelancers?

Shorter terms like Net 14 or Net 15 are often better for freelancers because they improve cash flow. Net 30 is fine for established business clients who expect it.
