How to Add a Payment Link to Your Invoice

Getting paid · Free BizDoc · __PUBDATE_EN__

A payment link turns “please transfer to this account” into a single tap to pay. It’s one of the simplest ways to get paid faster. Here is how they work and how to add one.

What a payment link is

A payment link is a URL that opens a ready-made checkout for a specific amount. The client clicks, pays by card or another method, and the money comes to you — no account numbers to copy, no bank app to open. On an invoice it usually appears as a “Pay now” button or link.

Why it gets you paid faster

Every step between “I’ll pay this” and the money arriving is a chance to delay. A link collapses those steps into one, so invoices with a pay-now option tend to settle noticeably sooner than bank-transfer-only ones.

How to create one

Payment links come from a payment processor — you create a link for the invoice amount and paste it onto the invoice, or use an invoicing tool that generates one for you. Set it to the exact total and reference the invoice number so you can match the payment later.

Fees and records

Card and processor fees apply — a small percentage per payment — so weigh that against getting paid faster, and build it into pricing if needed. When a link is paid, record it against the right invoice so your books stay reconciled.

Frequently asked questions

What is a payment link on an invoice?

A URL (often shown as a 'Pay now' button) that opens a checkout for the invoice amount. The client pays in one step, usually by card, instead of making a manual bank transfer.

Do payment links cost money?

The payment processor charges a small fee per transaction, typically a percentage of the amount. Many businesses accept that cost because links get invoices paid faster and reduce chasing.

How do I create a payment link?

Generate one from a payment processor for the exact invoice total, then add it to the invoice, or use an invoicing tool that creates the link for you. Reference the invoice number so you can reconcile the payment.
