How to Invoice as a Plumber (Labour, Parts &amp; Call-Outs)

Industries · Free BizDoc · __PUBDATE_EN__

A plumber’s invoice works best when it splits labour, parts and the call-out fee and ties to the job address. Clear breakdowns build trust and get you paid on the spot. Here is what to include.

What to include

  • Your business name, contact and any licence / registration number
  • The client and the job address (often not the billing address)
  • An itemised split: labour, parts/materials, and any call-out or emergency fee
  • Tax if you’re registered, a clear total, and payment details

Call-out and emergency fees

Plumbing often involves a call-out fee, and out-of-hours or emergency work commands a higher rate. Show these as their own line so the client sees exactly what the premium is for — a transparent emergency fee is accepted far more easily than a mysteriously large total.

Hourly, fixed, and deposits

Charge hourly plus parts for diagnostic or unpredictable work, and a fixed price for defined jobs (install a tap, fit a toilet). For larger jobs like a bathroom, take a deposit to cover materials and bill the balance on completion.

Get paid on site

Small domestic jobs get paid fastest when the invoice is handed over on completion, with an easy payment option — card on site or a payment link. Number every invoice and keep copies for your records and tax.

Frequently asked questions

What should a plumber's invoice include?

Your business and licence details, the client and job address, an itemised split of labour, parts and any call-out or emergency fee, tax if registered, a clear total and payment details.

Should I charge a call-out fee?

It's common to, especially for emergencies or out-of-hours work. Show it as its own line so the client sees what the fee covers, which makes it far easier to accept.

Should I take a deposit on big plumbing jobs?

For larger jobs with upfront material costs, yes &mdash; a deposit covers parts and the balance is due on completion. For quick call-outs, invoicing in full on the day is usually fine.
