What Is Making Tax Digital (MTD)?

Tax · Free BizDoc · __PUBDATE_EN__

Making Tax Digital (MTD) is the UK’s programme to move tax record-keeping and filing online. If you do business in the UK, it affects how you keep records. Here is a plain overview — check HMRC for the current detail.

What MTD is

MTD requires affected businesses to keep digital records and submit returns through compatible software rather than typing figures into a web form. The aim is fewer errors and a more real-time picture of tax. It has been rolling out in phases across different taxes.

Who it affects

MTD for VAT applies to VAT-registered businesses, and MTD for Income Tax is being phased in for self-employed people and landlords above set income levels. Thresholds and start dates have changed over time, so confirm where you fall with HMRC’s current guidance.

What it means for invoicing

Practically, you need your income and expense records in a digital, software-readable form — not a shoebox of paper or a stack of PDFs you re-type. Generating invoices digitally and keeping structured records makes MTD compliance much easier when returns are due.

Staying compliant

Use MTD-compatible software (or bridging software) to keep records and file, and keep your digital records current rather than scrambling at deadline. If you’re unsure whether and when MTD applies to you, HMRC’s guidance or an accountant is the place to confirm.
