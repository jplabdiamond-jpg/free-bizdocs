Invoicing a Business vs an Individual: What Changes

Invoicing · Free BizDoc · __PUBDATE_EN__

An invoice to a company and an invoice to a person are mostly the same document — but a few details change. Getting them right means a business invoice clears approval and gets paid. Here is what differs.

What stays the same

The core is identical: the word Invoice, a unique number, your details, an itemised list, the total, and how to pay. A clear, numbered invoice works for both. The differences are about how a business processes it.

What changes for a business

  • They may require a purchase order (PO) number on the invoice — without it, some firms won’t pay
  • They’ll want the invoice addressed to the correct legal entity and department
  • Longer payment terms are common (Net 30+)
  • Tax details may matter more (their VAT/tax number, reverse charge, etc.)
  • There’s usually an approval chain, so accuracy matters — errors restart it

Billing an individual

A private person usually just needs a clear invoice and an easy way to pay. Terms are often shorter, a payment link helps, and there’s no PO or approval chain — so the main lever is making payment effortless and the amount obvious.

Get the details right

For a business, ask up front who to address it to, whether they need a PO, and their terms. Those three questions prevent the most common cause of a late business invoice: it sat waiting because something required for their system was missing.

Frequently asked questions

Is invoicing a business different from invoicing a person?

The invoice itself is largely the same, but businesses often require a purchase order number, the correct legal entity, longer payment terms, and go through an approval process. Individuals usually just need a clear invoice and easy payment.

Do I need a PO number to invoice a company?

Some companies require one and won't pay an invoice without it. Ask up front whether they use purchase orders, and if so, put the PO number on the invoice.

Why do business invoices take longer to pay?

They typically pass through an approval chain and use longer terms like Net 30. Accurate invoices that meet their requirements (right entity, PO number) clear that process faster.
