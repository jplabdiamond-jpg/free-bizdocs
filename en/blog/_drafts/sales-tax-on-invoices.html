Do I Charge Sales Tax on Invoices? (US Guide)

Free BizDoc · __PUBDATE_EN__

“Do I add sales tax to this invoice?” is one of the most common questions US small businesses ask — and the honest answer is “it depends.” Sales tax in the US is set by states (and sometimes cities and counties), so the rules change with where you operate and what you sell. Here’s how to think about it.

There is no federal sales tax

Unlike VAT or GST in other countries, the US has no national sales tax. Each state sets its own rules and rates, and five states (Alaska, Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire and Oregon) have no statewide sales tax at all — though some localities in them do. So the first question is always: which state’s rules apply?

Do you have “nexus”?

You generally charge a state’s sales tax only if you have nexus there — a connection such as a physical presence (office, staff, inventory) or enough sales into the state to cross its economic-nexus threshold. If you have nexus in a state and sell something taxable to a customer there, you typically collect that state’s tax.

Are services taxable?

Physical goods are taxable in most states; services are where it gets tricky. Many states don’t tax professional services (design, consulting, writing), some tax specific services, and a few tax services broadly. Digital products and SaaS are treated differently again. Check your state’s guidance for your specific service.

How to show sales tax on an invoice

When you do charge it: show the taxable subtotal, the sales tax as a separate line with the rate, and the total. If some items are exempt and others aren’t, apply the rate per line. Keeping tax separate makes your invoice clear for the customer and clean for your books.

This is general information, not tax advice. Sales tax rules vary by state, city and industry and change over time — confirm your obligations with a tax professional or your state’s department of revenue.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to charge sales tax on every invoice?

No. In the US you generally charge a state’s sales tax only if you have nexus there and the item or service is taxable in that state. Many professional services aren’t taxable in many states.

Is sales tax charged on services?

It varies by state. Most states tax physical goods; some tax specific services, a few tax services broadly, and many exempt professional services. Check your state’s rules for your specific service.

How do I show sales tax on an invoice?

Show the taxable subtotal, then sales tax as a separate line with the rate, then the total. Apply the rate per line if some items are exempt.
