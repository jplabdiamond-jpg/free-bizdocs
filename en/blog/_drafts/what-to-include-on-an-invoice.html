What to Include on an Invoice: The Complete Checklist

Invoicing · Free BizDoc · __PUBDATE_EN__

A good invoice leaves nothing to guess. Miss a field and payment stalls while the client asks questions. Here is the complete checklist of what belongs on an invoice, from the essentials to the helpful extras.

The essentials

  • The word Invoice and a unique invoice number
  • Your business name and contact details
  • The client’s name and address
  • Issue date and due date
  • An itemised list: description, quantity, unit price, line total
  • Subtotal, any tax, and the total due
  • How to pay — bank details or a payment link

Tax details (if they apply)

If you are registered for VAT, GST or sales tax, add your tax number, the tax rate, and the tax amount, with totals before and after tax. Requirements differ by country — an Australian tax invoice, a UK VAT invoice and a US sales-tax invoice each have their own expected fields.

Helpful extras

  • Payment terms spelled out (e.g. “Due within 14 days”)
  • A purchase order or reference number if the client uses one
  • A short note of thanks
  • Late-fee terms, if you charge them
  • Your logo, for a professional look

What to leave off

Keep it clean. You don’t need a tax number you don’t have, and you shouldn’t bury the total under clutter. The reader should find the amount, due date and how to pay in seconds — everything else supports those three.

Frequently asked questions

What information must be on an invoice?

At minimum: the word invoice and a unique number, your details, the client's details, issue and due dates, an itemised list, the total, and how to pay. Add tax details if you are registered.

Does an invoice need a due date?

It should. A clear due date (or payment term like 'Net 14') tells the client exactly when payment is expected and tends to get invoices paid faster than leaving it open.

Do I need a logo on an invoice?

No, it is optional. A logo looks professional, but a clean, complete invoice without one is perfectly valid and will still get paid.
