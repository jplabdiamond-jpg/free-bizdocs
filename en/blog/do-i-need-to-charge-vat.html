Do I Need to Charge VAT? (UK &amp; EU Basics for Small Businesses)

Tax · Free BizDoc · Nov 5, 2026

Whether you charge VAT is not a choice — it depends on whether you are VAT registered. Here is when you must register, when you might choose to, and what changes on your invoices.

Registration is the deciding factor

You charge VAT only if you are registered for it. You must register once your taxable turnover passes the threshold in your country (in the UK this is a rolling 12-month figure). Below that, registration is optional. If you are not registered, you do not add VAT and your invoices carry no VAT line.

Should you register voluntarily?

  • For you: registering lets you reclaim VAT on your own purchases — useful if you buy a lot of taxable goods or services.
  • Against: it adds 20% (or your local rate) to prices, which can matter for consumer clients, plus extra admin and returns.
  • For mostly VAT-registered business clients, charging VAT is usually neutral, as they reclaim it.

What changes on your invoices

Once registered you must issue proper VAT invoices: your VAT number, the VAT rate and amount per line or in total, and the gross total. You then file VAT returns and pay over the VAT you collect, less the VAT you reclaim.

Cross-border sales

Selling to a VAT-registered business in another country often uses the reverse charge, where you add no VAT and the buyer accounts for it. Rules differ for goods, services and consumers, so confirm the specific case before you drop the tax.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to charge VAT?

Only if you are VAT registered. You must register once your taxable turnover exceeds your country's threshold; below it, registration is voluntary. If you are not registered, you do not add VAT to your invoices.

Is it worth registering for VAT voluntarily?

It can be, if you buy a lot of VAT-able goods and services or sell mainly to VAT-registered businesses that reclaim the tax. It is less attractive if your customers are consumers, because it effectively raises your prices.

Do I charge VAT to overseas business customers?

Often the reverse charge applies, meaning you add no VAT and the customer accounts for it. The treatment depends on goods vs services and the customer's location, so check the specific rule.
