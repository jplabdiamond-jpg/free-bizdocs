Invoice Payment Methods: Which to Offer and How to Get Paid Faster

Invoicing · Free BizDoc · Nov 11, 2026

The payment methods you offer affect how fast you get paid and how much you keep. Offering one easy option beats listing six — here is how the common methods compare and what to put on the invoice.

The common methods

  • Bank transfer — low or no fee, clears in 0–2 days; the default for B2B. Show account details clearly.
  • Card — instant and convenient, but you pay a processing fee (roughly 1.5–3%). Great for one-off or consumer clients.
  • Direct debit — ideal for recurring billing; the client authorises you to pull payment on the due date.
  • PayPal / wallets — fast and familiar, with a fee similar to cards.
  • Cash / cheque — still fine for local trades, but slower to record and reconcile.

Match the method to the client

For business clients, bank transfer is expected and cheap. For consumers or small jobs, a card or payment link removes friction and gets you paid on the spot. If you bill the same client every month, direct debit or saved-card recurring payments cut the chasing to zero.

Make paying effortless

  • Put the payment details on the invoice itself, not in a separate message.
  • Include everything needed in one place: account name, number, sort/routing code or IBAN, and a reference (your invoice number).
  • If you take cards, add a short pay link so the client can pay in two taps.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best payment method for invoices?

For business clients, bank transfer is usually best: it is cheap and expected. For consumers or small one-off jobs, offering a card or payment link gets you paid faster. Pick one clear default rather than listing many options.

Should I pass card fees on to the client?

You can add a surcharge where it is legal and disclosed, but many businesses absorb the small fee because faster payment is worth it. Check the rules in your country before surcharging.

How do I get paid faster?

Put complete payment details on the invoice, offer a frictionless option like a card link, set a clear due date, and send the invoice promptly. The easier it is to pay, the sooner you are paid.
