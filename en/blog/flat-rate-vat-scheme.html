What Is the Flat Rate VAT Scheme?

Tax · Free BizDoc · Nov 20, 2026

The UK’s Flat Rate VAT Scheme simplifies VAT for small businesses: instead of tracking VAT on every purchase, you pay a fixed percentage of turnover. Here is how it works and whether it suits you.

How it works

Normally you charge VAT, reclaim VAT on purchases, and pay the difference. Under the flat rate scheme you still charge VAT as usual, but you pay HMRC a fixed percentage of your gross turnover and generally don’t reclaim VAT on purchases (with some exceptions for larger capital assets). The percentage depends on your trade sector.

Who it can suit

It can suit small businesses with low VATable costs — service businesses that don’t buy much with VAT on it — because the flat percentage may be less than the VAT they’d otherwise hand over. It also means simpler record-keeping, since you’re not tracking input VAT on every receipt.

The trade-offs

  • You generally can’t reclaim VAT on purchases, so it’s poor for businesses that buy a lot of VATable goods
  • There are eligibility limits on turnover
  • A special lower-benefit category applies to “limited cost” businesses

Is it worth it?

It depends entirely on your costs and sector percentage. For some small service businesses it saves money and admin; for others it costs more than standard VAT. The rules, rates and limits are set by HMRC and change over time, so check the current detail and run your own numbers before joining.
