How to Follow Up on an Unpaid Invoice (With Email Templates)

Getting paid · Free BizDoc · Nov 5, 2026

Most late payments are oversight, not refusal. A calm, scheduled follow-up gets the majority paid without friction. Here is a schedule and the wording to use at each step.

A simple follow-up schedule

  • Due date — a short “invoice due today” reminder.
  • +3 to 7 days — a friendly nudge assuming it was missed.
  • +14 days — a firmer reminder stating the overdue amount and any late fee.
  • +30 days — a final notice with a clear deadline and next step.

Keep the tone right

Stay polite and factual — assume good faith until the pattern says otherwise. Always include the invoice number, amount and due date, re-attach the invoice, and make it effortless to pay by repeating the payment details. One clear ask per email.

Wording you can copy

Friendly nudge: Hi [name], just a quick reminder that invoice [#] for [amount] was due on [date]. I have re-attached it — could you let me know when it will be paid? Thanks!
Final notice: Hi [name], invoice [#] for [amount] is now 30 days overdue. Please arrange payment by [date]. If there is an issue with the invoice, tell me and I will help resolve it.

When reminders do not work

If a final notice passes with no reply, consider pausing further work, adding a late fee you disclosed in your terms, or sending a formal demand. Keep a written record of every reminder in case you need it later.

Frequently asked questions

How soon should I chase an unpaid invoice?

Send a brief reminder on the due date, then a friendly nudge a few days later. Waiting weeks only trains clients to pay late. Prompt, polite reminders get most invoices paid.

How often should I send reminders?

A reasonable rhythm is due date, roughly one week, two weeks, and a final notice at 30 days. Space them out, keep each one short, and always include the invoice number and amount.

Can I charge a late fee?

Yes, if you stated it in your payment terms before the work. A common approach is a small percentage per month overdue. Mention the fee in your firmer reminders.
