An invoice is the document you send to request payment for goods or services. This free invoice generator builds a clean, professional PDF with everything an accounts-payable team expects: a unique invoice number, issue and due dates, itemized lines, tax, the total due and your payment details.

What to include on an invoice

  • Your business name, address and contact details - and your tax ID where required (VAT number in the UK, ABN in Australia, GST/HST number in Canada).
  • The word “Invoice”, a unique sequential number and the issue date
  • The client’s name and address (“Bill To”)
  • Line items with description, quantity, unit price and amount
  • Subtotal, tax and total - shown per tax rate where more than one applies
  • Payment terms, due date and how to pay (bank details, routing / sort code / BSB, etc.)

Country-specific rules this tool handles

  • United States - there is no federal invoice standard; sales tax depends on your state and the type of service. Set a rate per line or mark items exempt. “Net 30” is the most common payment term.
  • United Kingdom - VAT-registered businesses must show their VAT registration number, the VAT rate (20%, 5% or 0%) and the VAT amount per rate, plus the total VAT in GBP.
  • Australia - a tax invoice must show the words “Tax Invoice”, your ABN, the date, a description, the GST amount (10%) and, for sales of $1,000 or more, the buyer’s identity. The title switches to “Tax Invoice” automatically when you enter an ABN and charge GST.
  • Canada - choose your province and GST, HST, PST or QST are applied at the correct rates; your GST/HST number is printed on the invoice.
Tax rules change and vary by state, province and industry. The calculations here are an aid - confirm your obligations with your accountant or tax authority.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free, with no sign-up?

Yes. You can create and download invoices without an account. To keep the service fast for everyone, the free version allows 2 PDF downloads per hour from the same network.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. The invoice content is never sent to our servers - the PDF is generated in your browser. Only your own business details are kept in your browser local storage so you do not have to retype them.

Does it support VAT, GST and sales tax?

Yes. Pick United States (a sales tax rate you set), United Kingdom (VAT 20% / 5% / 0% / exempt), Australia (GST 10% / GST-free) or Canada (GST / HST / PST / QST by province). Tax is itemized per rate on the PDF.

What paper size is the PDF?

US and Canada use Letter (8.5 x 11 in). UK and Australia use A4.

Can I turn an estimate into an invoice?

Yes. Under the preview, click the document you want - the same details are reused with a new number and today’s date.

Can I send the invoice without attaching a PDF?

Yes. “Create a share link” encodes the document in a URL. Your client opens it in a browser, sees the amount, due date and payment details, and can download the PDF.
