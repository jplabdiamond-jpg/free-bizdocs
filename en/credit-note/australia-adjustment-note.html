Frequently asked questions

What is an adjustment note?

In Australia a GST credit note is called an adjustment note. It reduces or cancels a tax invoice - for a return, cancellation or price change - and reverses the GST.

When do I issue one?

When the price of a taxable sale changes after you issued the tax invoice (a return, cancellation or discount). It references the original tax invoice and reverses the GST.
