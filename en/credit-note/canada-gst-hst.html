What a Canada credit note must include

  • Your business name and your GST/HST number
  • A unique credit note number and the date
  • The original invoice number it relates to
  • The lines being credited, with the GST/HST reversed by province
  • The total credit in CAD

Frequently asked questions

Does it reverse GST/HST?

Yes - the GST/HST (and PST/QST where it applies) charged on the original invoice is reversed on the credit note at your province’s rate.

Do I show my GST/HST number?

Yes - as on the original invoice, so the customer can adjust their input tax credits.
