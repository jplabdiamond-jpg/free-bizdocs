What a United Kingdom credit note must include

  • Your business name and VAT registration number
  • A unique credit note number and the date
  • The original invoice number it relates to
  • The lines being credited, with the VAT reversed at the original rate
  • The total credit excluding VAT, the VAT, and the gross in GBP

Frequently asked questions

What must a UK VAT credit note show?

Your VAT number, a unique number, the date, the original invoice reference, a description, and the VAT reversed at the rate originally charged. Keep it for your VAT records.

How does it affect my VAT return?

A VAT credit note reduces the output VAT you declared on the original invoice. Record it against the period in which it is issued.
