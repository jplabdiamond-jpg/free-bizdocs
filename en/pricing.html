Frequently asked questions

Is the free version free?

Yes. You can create and download documents without an account. The free version allows 2 PDF downloads per hour from the same network, which is plenty for occasional use.

What exactly do I get with PRO?

Unlimited PDF downloads (the hourly limit is removed) and the ability to save your documents to your account and reopen or duplicate them later from any device.

Is there a yearly plan?

Switch the toggle to “Yearly” to see the annual rate — it works out to 2 months free versus paying monthly. To subscribe annually, contact us and we’ll set it up.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel from the billing portal at any time; your PRO access continues until the end of the current billing period. No long-term contract.

Which currency am I charged in?

PRO is billed in US dollars (USD) via Stripe. Your card issuer handles any currency conversion.

Do you store my invoice contents?

Only if you're signed in and choose "Save to account". Documents you create without saving are generated entirely in your browser and never reach our servers. See our Privacy Policy.

Already subscribed?

Manage your subscription, update your card or cancel from the Stripe billing portal using the email you subscribed with.
