Which should you choose?

Pick Free BizDoc if…

  • You want a professional PDF right now, with no account.
  • You invoice occasionally and don’t need full accounting.
  • You’re in the US, UK, Australia or Canada and want the tax handled.
  • You’d rather your data stay in your browser, not a cloud account.

Pick a full platform if…

  • You need to collect card payments inside the invoice.
  • You want recurring billing, reminders and a client portal.
  • You want invoicing tied to full accounting (Wave, Zoho).
  • You’re happy to create an account and manage data in their cloud.

Frequently asked questions

Which free invoice tool doesn’t require sign-up?

Free BizDoc lets you create and download an invoice PDF without an account. Invoice Simple, Wave, Zoho Invoice and Invoice Ninja all require you to register before you can create or download a document.

Is Wave really free?

Wave’s invoicing and basic bookkeeping are free, and its paid Pro plan (around $19/month) adds features like automated reminders and custom branding. Online payments carry transaction fees. You do need a Wave account.

Is Zoho Invoice free forever?

Yes — Zoho Invoice has a genuinely free plan with limits (for example a cap on users and invoices per year). It requires a Zoho account, and heavier needs move you to the paid Zoho Books.

What’s the catch with Free BizDoc being free?

There isn’t a hidden one. The core document tools are free with no sign-up; the free version limits you to 2 PDF downloads per hour. An optional PRO plan ($15/month) removes the limit and adds saving and basic management. Documents are generated in your browser and aren’t stored unless you choose to save them.
